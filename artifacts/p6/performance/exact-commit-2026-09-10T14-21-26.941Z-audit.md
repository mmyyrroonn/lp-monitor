## Remaining growth mechanisms observed in source

`commitAcceptedSignalBatch` accepts the raw batch, calls `SqliteProjectionStore.rebuild`, then `projectSignals`. P2 rebuild snapshots all current logs, times, registrations and accepted ranges, replays all retained events, deletes all scope projection detail rows, reinserts the entire result and serializes its cursor. The subsequent metrics read rebuilds that full source snapshot again to prove freshness before decoding the persisted projection. Across the complete measured call there were six `activeLogs`, six `logTimes`, eight `pools`, and two projection `snapshot` calls; these counts include nested work and must not be summed with inclusive phase timers.

Metrics coverage still reads and fingerprints every selected batch's payload and shards on each report, despite avoiding repeated successful shard decode. Signal projection rereads retained evidence, hashes logs/registrations, evaluates every pool and persists state changes. The separate `buildMetricsReport` measurement therefore omitted material P2/raw/signal/commit work; it was insufficient by itself to establish end-to-end margin.

Next investigation candidates are narrower than a historical/incremental rewrite: measure repeated source snapshot work within this one already-atomic transaction, P2 decoding/signature derivation, and coverage SQL execution/fingerprinting separately. No optimization is implemented by this audit, and freshness, reorg, source mutation, signal evidence and all latency samples must remain authoritative.
